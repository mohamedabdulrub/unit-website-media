Franchise FAQ

How much does a Unit franchise cost?

Investment starts from £100,000 for a Truck Unit, £250,000 for a Large Unit (60 to 80 covers) and £400,000 for an XL Unit (up to 110 covers). The final cost depends on the condition of the location, planning permission and other factors. Send us an enquiry for the full breakdown in our franchise information pack.

What support do franchisees get?

End-to-end support covering training, site search and acquisition, design and build, launch marketing, ongoing marketing, EPOS and operations manuals.

Who does the Unit menu appeal to?

Everyone. Alongside burgers, wings and loaded fries, the menu covers vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free diets, and it's fully halal, so groups and families can all eat together.

What happens after I enquire?

We'll send you more information and arrange a phone call. If it's a good fit, we'll invite you to a Discovery Day to meet the team and see Unit in action.

Important information

Your capital is at risk. Investing in a franchise involves risk, and you could lose some or all of the money you invest. Past performance of Unit's own restaurants is not a reliable indicator of how any franchised outlet will perform, and there is no guarantee of profit, turnover or a return on your investment.

All investment figures on this page are indicative starting costs only. They are not a quote or an offer, and the final cost depends on the location, its condition, planning permission, fit-out requirements and other factors. Additional costs such as working capital, fees and taxes may apply.

Nothing on this page is financial, legal or tax advice, or an offer of a franchise. Any franchise is subject to contract: the terms will be set out in a Franchise Agreement and Franchise Information Memorandum, which you should review carefully before committing.

We strongly recommend you take independent legal and financial advice before entering into any agreement, ideally from a solicitor and accountant experienced in franchising, such as an affiliated professional adviser of the British Franchise Association.

Franchise opportunities are not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). You will not have access to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and your investment is not protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).

Street art at Headford Street

That's the Spirit, 30 metres of it

We got Sheffield's own Kid Acne in to paint a 30 metre THAT'S THE SPIRIT along the wall outside Headford Street, hand-lettered in his signature black and white. You can't miss it from the car park.

He also designed our logo, so every time you see the UNIT badge, that's Kid Acne too.

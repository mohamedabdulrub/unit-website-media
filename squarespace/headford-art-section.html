New on the walls

A new mural by Enso and Jim McElvaney

Sheffield artists Enso and Jim McElvaney have hand-painted a huge new piece inside Headford Street: a pale skull figure set in a sunburst of black and gold calligraphy, painted straight onto the wooden walls.

Street art has been part of Unit since day one. Come and see it in person, grab a booth next to it, and tag the artists when you post.

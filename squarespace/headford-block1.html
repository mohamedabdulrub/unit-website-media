Headford Street questions

Where can I eat near Bramall Lane?

Unit Headford Street is within walking distance of Bramall Lane, home of Sheffield United. We serve burgers, wings, loaded fries and shakes made fresh to order. Book a table on match days, or order for collection.

Where can I get food near the University of Sheffield?

Unit Headford Street is a short walk from the University of Sheffield campus around West Street, open 11am to 11pm every day, for eat in, takeaway and collection.

Where can I eat near Sheffield Hallam University?

Unit Headford Street is just down the road from Sheffield Hallam's Collegiate Campus in Broomhall, and a short walk from City Campus across the city centre.

Where can I get late-night food in Sheffield city centre?

Unit Headford Street is open until 11pm every day, close to Division Street and West Street. Last orders are 15 minutes before closing.

Is there parking at Unit Headford Street?

There is pay and display parking right outside, and it is free after 8:30pm.

Can I book a table for a group or society night?

Yes. We have big booths and take group bookings. For groups of 10 or more, please give us 24 hours' notice.

Do you have gluten-free burgers in Sheffield city centre?

Yes. Any of our burgers can be served in a gluten-free bun, or without a bun, just ask when you order. Our kitchen handles gluten and we can't rule out cross-contamination, so we don't recommend it for coeliacs. Ask our team for allergen information.

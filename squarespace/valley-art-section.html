Melville x Unit: our hallway, covered

Sheffield artist Melville has turned our hallway into one massive piece: layer after layer of torn paste-ups of tower blocks, graffiti, street signs, vans and CCTV warnings, collaged floor to ceiling.

Take a slow walk through on your way in. There's something new to spot every time, so tag the artist when you post it.

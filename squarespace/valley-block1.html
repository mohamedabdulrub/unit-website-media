Valley Centertainment questions

Where can I eat near Utilita Arena Sheffield?

Unit Valley Centertainment is on Broughton Lane, the same road as Utilita Arena Sheffield, a short walk away. We serve burgers, wings, loaded fries and shakes made fresh to order, with free parking. Book ahead on event nights.

Where should I eat before a Sheffield Steelers game?

Come to Unit Valley Centertainment before the game, then walk to Utilita Arena. Book a table on Steelers home game nights, or order for collection if you are short on time.

Are there restaurants near Meadowhall?

Yes. Unit Valley Centertainment is a few minutes from Meadowhall, with free parking. It is an independent American diner serving burgers, wings and shakes.

Where can I eat near IKEA Sheffield?

Unit Valley Centertainment is a short drive from IKEA Sheffield in Tinsley, at the Valley Centertainment leisure park with free parking.

Is there parking at Unit Valley Centertainment?

Yes, Valley Centertainment has a free car park. The Valley Centertainment tram stop is also right outside.

What time does Unit Valley Centertainment close?

We are open Sunday to Thursday 10am to 10pm, and Friday and Saturday 10am to 11pm. Last orders are 15 minutes before closing.

Is Unit Valley Centertainment good for families and groups?

Yes. It is our biggest dining room, with big booths, a kids menu and vegan options, next to the cinema and bowling.

Heading into town instead? Our original diner is on Headford Street in Sheffield city centre.

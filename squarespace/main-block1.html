Where street food and street art meet

Mo and Nabz were fed up with Sheffield's food options, so they quit their jobs to make food they crave. Unit opened on Headford Street in 2017 as a home for students, families and local creatives, with walls by local and international graffiti artists. Today there are two Unit diners in Sheffield, and we're still independent.

Questions we get asked

Is Unit Sheffield halal?

Yes. Everything we serve is halal, at both Headford Street and Valley Centertainment.

Where can I get good burgers in Sheffield city centre?

Unit's original diner is at 88 Headford Street, S3 7WB, a short walk from Devonshire Green and Division Street. It's open 11am to 11pm, seven days a week.

Do I need to book?

No, walk-ins are welcome. We recommend booking a table for groups and Friday or Saturday evenings.

Do you have vegan and vegetarian options?

Yes: vegan cauliflower wings in your choice of flavour, the Moving Mountains vegan beef burger and our homemade falafel burger. Vegetarians can also go for the halloumi burger, halloumi fries or our homemade mac n cheese.

Do you have gluten-free food?

Most burgers can be made without the bun; just ask. We can't guarantee no cross-contamination, so we don't recommend it for coeliacs.

Is there parking?

At Headford Street there's pay and display right outside, free after 8:30pm. Valley Centertainment has free parking.

Do you deliver?

Yes, from both diners via Uber Eats.

Can you take large group bookings in Sheffield?

Yes. We seat big groups together, take pre-orders so the food arrives at once, and sort dietary needs in advance. Valley Centertainment is our biggest dining room. Book a group.

Do you do working lunches and office orders?

Yes, with packages for 5, 10, 20 and 50+ people. With 24 hours' notice, we can deliver large orders up to 4 miles (delivery charges apply beyond 1 mile). See Unit for Business.

Is Unit good for kids and families?

Yes. We have a kids menu, and our Centertainment diner sits right by the cinema and bowling.

What time are last orders?

15 minutes before closing at every location.

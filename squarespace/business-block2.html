Business ordering FAQ

Can Unit cater a working lunch in Sheffield?

Yes. We make working lunches, team lunches and office orders for groups of 5 to 50+, from our diners on Headford Street (S3) and at Valley Centertainment (S9).

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. We have vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options, and our whole menu is halal, so the whole team is covered. Just tell us what you need when you order.

How much notice do you need?

For groups of 10 or more, we ask for 24 hours' notice. Need something sooner? Email or call your nearest Unit at least an hour before and we'll check what we can do. Our sharing platters can be ordered for collection the same day.

Do you deliver to offices?

Yes. With 24 hours' notice, we deliver large orders up to 4 miles from either diner. Delivery charges apply beyond 1 mile. Orders can always be collected instead.

Can we book a big table for a team social?

Yes. We'll seat your group together, take pre-orders so everyone's food arrives at once, and handle dietary requirements in advance.

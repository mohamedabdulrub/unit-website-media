Questions

Do you deliver from Centertainment?

Yes, on Uber Eats. Search Miss Drippy Desserts – Centertainment in the app.

Yes, Valley Centertainment has free parking, and the tram stops right outside.

Yes, we're on Broughton Lane, the same road as Utilita Arena. Grab dessert before or after the show.

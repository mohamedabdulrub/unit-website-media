Behind the brand

There are four of us, living and working in Sheffield. We make cola. We are not a multinational, we have no investors, and we have no marketing department. Just a better bottle of cola.

Why we made Wisl

A cola we actually wanted to drink

We weren’t trying to reinvent anything. We just wanted a cola that tastes really nice. Something that feels like a proper treat, for us and for everyone else who picks one up.

With a burger

Ice-cold, next to something properly good to eat. The way a cola should be.

Instead of another coffee

In the coffee shop when you don’t fancy a coffee, but you still want to sit down and get some work done.

Catching up with friends

Something a bit special on the table when you meet up and lose track of time.
